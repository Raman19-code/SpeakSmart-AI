
# 🎙️ SpeakSmart AI

> **Your AI-powered voice partner for Group Discussions, Debates, and Spoken English Practice.**

SpeakSmart AI is a voice-based AI assistant that helps students improve their English communication skills through interactive Group Discussions (GD) and debate practice. Instead of simply correcting grammar, SpeakSmart AI actively participates in conversations, challenges opinions, and provides personalized feedback to help users become more confident speakers.

---

## 🚀 Features

* 🎤 **Voice-Based Conversations**

  * Natural voice interaction using Vapi.
  * Hands-free speaking practice.

* 🗣️ **Group Discussion Practice**

  * Practice GDs on real-world topics.
  * AI acts as a discussion participant or moderator.

* ⚖️ **Debate Mode**

  * AI can take the opposite viewpoint.
  * Encourages critical thinking and structured arguments.

* ✍️ **Grammar Correction**

  * Identifies grammatical mistakes after the discussion.
  * Explains corrections with examples.

* 📝 **Sentence Improvement**

  * Suggests more natural and professional sentence structures.

* 📚 **Vocabulary Enhancement**

  * Recommends stronger words and expressions.
  * Helps reduce repetitive language.

* 🔥 **Trending GD Topics**

  * Suggests current and relevant discussion topics.
  * Covers technology, business, education, politics, and social issues.

* 💬 **Constructive Feedback**

  * Highlights strengths and areas for improvement.
  * Encourages confident and fluent communication.

## 📌 Example

**User:**

> I think AI will replace software engineers because AI can write code.

**SpeakSmart AI:**

> That's an interesting perspective. However, software engineering also involves system design, collaboration, and decision-making. Could you explain why you believe AI can fully replace engineers?

**Feedback**

* Grammar: ✅ Good
* Vocabulary Suggestion:

  * "replace" → "transform" (depending on context)
* Improvement:

  > *I believe AI will significantly transform software engineering by automating repetitive coding tasks.*
